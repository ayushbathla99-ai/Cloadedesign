## 1. Her topic architecture (the 6 pillars of GTM + 2 layers)

Maja's whole body of work hangs on her book's six GTM elements, plus two layers added over time:

```
            ┌──────────── AI-NATIVE LAYER (era 3) ────────────┐
            │ Claude Code · agents · context engineering · AEO │
            └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
  MARKET → ICP → PRODUCT → POSITIONING → PRICING → GROWTH (motions)
            └──────────── PROOF LAYER ─────────────────────────┘
              case studies · State-of data · her own launches
```

**Why it works:** every post plugs into a known slot, so readers learn a *system* over time, not random tips. Do the same: pick your pillars once and rotate through them.

## 5. Distribution loop (copying her LinkedIn → newsletter engine)

1. **Publish the article** (LinkedIn article or newsletter) on a fixed day. She ships every Friday.
2. **Day 0:** a native LinkedIn *post* with the strongest hook + 3 bullets + "full breakdown in the article".
3. **Day 2:** a carousel or visual of the named framework (the most shareable piece).
4. **Day 4:** a story-post from the article's opening anecdote.
5. **Day 6:** a data or quote snippet + reader question.
6. Collect replies, then turn them into a next-week article or a mini-survey (she does this constantly: "after tremendous engagement… my assumption is that you are up for more tactical posts").
