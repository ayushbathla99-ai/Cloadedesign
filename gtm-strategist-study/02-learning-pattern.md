## 1. The one-sentence model

> **Pick one GTM decision your reader is stuck on → show proof you've been in the trenches → hand them a named framework + a template they can run this week → point to a bigger asset.**

Every post is a variation of that loop. Topics change with the market, but the loop stays the same.

### The five engagement drivers (in order of strength)

1. **Own a term.** ECP, GTM Power Hour, Market-Problem Map, GTM Actions vs Motions, Lean ABM, Prompt-Led engine, GTM Harness, Living Content Graph. Naming the thing makes it shareable and attributable.
2. **Give away a working artifact.** Repo, Miro board, template, prompt, checklist, scorecard, sales deck. People react to things they can *use*.
3. **Proprietary numbers.** "I analyzed 199 LinkedIn posts", "3,073 posts from 30 creators", "195 companies", "228 respondents", "400+ launches", "12,000+ companies". Research volume = credibility.
4. **Address a fresh fear.** AI killing SEO, LinkedIn reach collapsing, AI SDRs, margins dropping to 50%. Name the fear, then calm it with a system.
5. **Stage-specific guidance.** "Before ideal there is early", milestones, "you're scaling at the wrong time". Readers want to know *where they are*.

Lowest performers: pure promotions (bootcamp, Black Friday, webinar invites) and sponsor-led tool roundups. **Lesson for you: never make the promo the post. Make the post, then attach the promo.**

## 4. How each post teaches (the micro-pattern)

Across the corpus, a post moves the reader through six beats:

1. **Situate**: "Dear GTM Strategist!" plus a timely trigger (a client call, a trip, a market change, a reader question).
2. **Tension**: the mistake, the myth, or the uncomfortable truth ("And there it is. The sentence that kills more launches than I'd care to admit.").
3. **Credibility**: why *I* can speak on this (N companies, a named guest operator, data).
4. **Promise**: "In this post, you will learn:" with 3–5 bold bullets.
5. **Teach in modules**: lesson → framework → tool/template → example, repeated per section.
6. **Land and extend**: "Where I stand:" verdict, a reflective question to the reader, a P.S. with the next event or asset, and a standing resource footer.

This is a **learn → apply → own** pattern. Readers never leave with just an idea; they leave with an *action and an artifact*.

## 5. Operating cadence and business model (useful for your own system)

- **Weekly, every Friday**, without exception for ~3 years (52 posts in 2024 alone).
- **Freemium:** 142/146 posts are paid; the free intro is engineered to sell the click. The intro (first ~150–250 words) does all the hook work.
- **Guest leverage:** ~1 in 10 posts is a guest operator ("Over to Kyle."). It brings borrowed authority, a cross-audience, and less writing load.
- **Co-created data:** recurring State-of reports with Kyle Poyar (Growth Unhinged) give them proprietary data and two audiences.
- **Sponsors:** single-sponsor blurbs from 2024, then a standing "Presenting partner + GTM partners" block in 2026. Sponsors are written in first person, as an endorsement ("My score was 79% AI Ready. Can you beat me?").
- **Asset ladder footer** on every post: checklist → book → course → masterclass → work-with-me.
- **LinkedIn is the distribution engine:** her stated numbers are 74K+ followers, 7M+ yearly impressions, 70% of pipeline. The newsletter is the depth layer and LinkedIn is the reach layer.
